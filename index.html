Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Learn how ByajX Pro handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ByajX Pro ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the ByajX Pro mobile application.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use and the information you enter, the app may process:

  • Sign-in information: information made available through Google Sign-In, such as your name, email address, and profile image, subject to the permissions and account settings you approve.
  • Records you enter: customer names, contact details, principal amounts, interest rates, payment entries, due dates, notes, and related account records that you choose to add.
  • Technical information: limited app, device, diagnostic, and security information where required to operate, maintain, and protect the service.

Please do not enter information that you do not have permission to store.

2. How we use information

Information is used to provide Google sign-in, maintain your records, calculate and display interest-related information, support app features, synchronize data, provide support, and help protect the app against misuse.

3. Firebase and data storage

ByajX Pro uses Google Firebase services for authentication and, where configured in the app, online storage and synchronization. Some app data may also be stored locally on your device so supported features can work offline. When an internet connection becomes available, locally stored changes may synchronize with the online service.

Data stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. The exact behavior can depend on your device, app version, and synchronization status.

4. Google Sign-In

You can sign in using your Google account. Google provides the authentication service and shares the basic profile information permitted by the sign-in flow. We use this information to identify your account and provide access to the app. Google's handling of information is also governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers such as Google Firebase when needed to provide sign-in, hosting, storage, synchronization, security, or app functionality. We may also disclose information if required by applicable law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the service.

6. Data security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. You should keep your Google account secure and protect access to your device.

7. Data retention and deletion

We retain account and app information for as long as needed to provide the service and for legitimate operational or legal purposes. You may request deletion of your ByajX Pro account and associated data by emailing byajxpro@gmail.com from your registered email address. Include the subject line “ByajX Pro – Account and Data Deletion Request” and the Google account email used in the app.

We will verify the request before processing it. Some information may remain in backups for a limited period or be retained where required by law. Deleting the app from your device may not, by itself, delete information already stored online.

8. Children's privacy

ByajX Pro is not intended for children under the age at which they can legally provide consent to use the service in their location. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Your choices

You can choose what information to enter into the app. You can also stop using the app and submit a deletion request using the contact details below. You may manage Google account permissions through your Google Account settings.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
